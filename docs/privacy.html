Privacy

Foolscap looks at nothing, records nothing, and phones home for nothing.

There are no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no accounts, and no advertising identifiers. Foolscap collects no data about you — not your documents, not your habits, not your machine. There is no server on our side for anything to be sent to.

Your documents are plain files on your disk. They are read when you open them, written when you save, and never leave your computer. Everything you write is yours and only yours.

The whole disclosure, for completeness: the direct-download edition asks GitHub once in a while whether a newer version of Foolscap exists — a request that carries no personal information beyond what any web request carries, answered by GitHub’s ordinary release infrastructure. The Mac App Store edition doesn’t even do that; the App Store handles its updates. Links you click in a document open in your own browser. That is the complete list of network activity.

Since Foolscap is open source, none of this needs to be taken on faith: read the source.

Effective August 2026. If this policy ever changes, the change will be visible in the repository history — but the promise above is the point of the app, so don’t hold your breath. · Back to Foolscap
